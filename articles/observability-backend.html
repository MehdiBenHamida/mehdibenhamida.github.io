Observability for Backend Services

Observability becomes valuable when it supports decisions, not when it generates more dashboards than the team can interpret. The goal is operational clarity.

Logs tell the story

Structured logs make it possible to trace user journeys, diagnose failures, and connect operational events back to business workflows.

Metrics show system shape

Metrics provide the quick signal: latency, error rates, throughput, queue depth, and saturation. They help teams decide where deeper investigation is needed.

Traces connect the path

In distributed systems, traces reveal how requests move across services. They help expose the true cost of dependencies and where the critical path actually lives.
