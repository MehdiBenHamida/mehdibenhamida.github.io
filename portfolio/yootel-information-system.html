Project

YOOTEL Information System

At Yootel, I designed and deployed an information system based on microservices, with a strong emphasis on customer-facing workflows and operational efficiency.

Challenge

The company needed a cohesive internal platform that could support dashboards, billing, service configuration, and centralized data usage across products.

Approach

I designed the system architecture, implemented services and APIs, created a centralized data warehouse, and set up CI/CD automation to accelerate delivery.

Outcome

The platform reduced time-to-market, improved customer operations, and provided a stronger technical base for future service expansion.
