Project

Gorgias Phone Platform Refactoring

I led a significant refactor of the Gorgias phone solution to improve reliability while preserving service continuity in production.

Challenge

The platform needed better resilience and performance, but the migration path had to avoid user-facing downtime.

Approach

I applied refactoring patterns, improved database-related performance, and defined a migration strategy that could be rolled out incrementally without interrupting users.

Outcome

The work resulted in stronger system reliability, reduced degradation, and a zero-downtime transition path for the production environment.
